### The instrument

*register-erased shape* (opcode, function code, shift amount, immediate and
branch offset kept; every GPR and FPR selector erased, including COP1 `fs`/`ft`/
`fd`, which the ranking's own `instr_reg_mask` deliberately leaves alone), then
classify each aligned pair as byte-exact, register-naming-only, or genuinely
different. The three buckets partition the residual by *cause*:

Three of these were fixed by source changes; the fixes are independent and
each was measured alone.

**1. The header hunk (worth 311 positional words).** With `object->unk8D`
assigned after `object->unk91`/`unk93`, the scheduler has two independent byte
stores in reach of the earlier floating-point hazard and fills it; the target
emits the hazard `nop` instead and uses the same two stores to cover the *later*
byte-load delay. Moving the `unk8D` assignment ahead of the two zeroing stores
reproduces the target's choice. Measured alone this *grows* the object by one
word (719 -> 720), which is the point: it restores an instruction the candidate
was missing, and reveals that two other instructions were surplus.

**2. The nested pointer fixups (worth 2 instructions and ~180 words).** Both
`unk4C` and `unk48` fixups test an inner field and then recompute from it. The
candidate re-read the field inside the body, giving one load more than the
target, which reuses the value it tested. Holding it in a carrier (the dead
`offset` local; a fresh local was not needed) removes the reload. A
six-carrier x two-operand-order x three-site-set lattice (72 compiled and
scored candidates) ranks `offset` first at 187, `index`/`resultSize`/`size` at
189 and `loadType` at 287; applying it to *both* sites overshoots to a -4 size
delta, so exactly one carrier is right and the second surplus instruction is
still unlocated.

**3. `resultSize = 0;` before `size >>= 2;`** is worth one further word (187 ->
186) and one more aligned-exact row.

### Next lever

Locate the second surplus instruction (the one that makes the two-carrier form
overshoot) at the copy-loop preamble around +0x6DC, then re-test the two-site
carrier form. Two handles on it are already measured and closed. The missing
word is the scaled induction variable: the target computes `resultSize * 4` as
a real shift of a register that holds zero, and the candidate folds it away.
Spelling it `resultSize << 2` instead of `resultSize * 4` is **completely
inert** --- byte-identical objects at both sites, across every other variant
tried. Moving the `resultSize = 0;` definition to any of four earlier anchors
does restore the instruction (size delta returns to +4, and to 0 once the
second fixup carrier is added), but it costs far more than it buys: the first
mismatch retreats from +0x1B4 to +0xAC or +0xB8, breaking the exact opening 110
words, and the score lands at 547-624 against 186. The handle must therefore
block the fold without moving the definition. After that the residual is entirely saved-register and temp-ring
naming, and the axis for the saved pair is *not* declaration order --- that is
measured, not assumed.
