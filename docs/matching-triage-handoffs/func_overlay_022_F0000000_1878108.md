- one extern's address read at three call sites in three basic blocks emits

- two reads inside one basic block emit **one** for that block, and the other

- adding a fourth read never produces a stack temporary.

So **uopt does not merge an address constant across a basic-block boundary at
all** -- there is no web to spill, and no source arrangement of a plain
address constant produces one. The only construct that yields the target's

**The two facts are in tension and that is the finding.** The census says five
declared names; the rematerialisation law says the single materialisation needs
