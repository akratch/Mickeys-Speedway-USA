### The exchanged saved pair, and the first divergence

The first divergence at +0x88 is now readable as a single fact: **the target
stores the 32-byte pool count in a caller-saved temporary (`t3`), the candidate
stores it in `s2`.** Because `s2` is then defined inside the entry block, its
stack save is scheduled one slot earlier than the target's, and that one slot is
the whole +0x88 displacement. The instruction the candidate emits at +0x88 is
its own `s2` save; the target is publishing an order record there and does not
save `s2` until later.

**It is not an extra saved register.** A typed stack-home census shows 12
distinct homes and 24 accesses on *each* side, at identical offsets, with
identical widths, and both frames are 0x50. The two sides save the same
registers into the same slots; only the position of one save differs.

The register census over the whole function:

- target `s3` where the candidate uses `s2`: 51 sites
- target `s2` where the candidate uses `s3`: 37 sites
- target `t3`, `a1`, `t2` or `t4` where the candidate uses `s2`: 41 sites

The first two rows are the exchanged pair. The third is the mechanism: **41
sites where the target uses a caller-saved register and the candidate uses
`s2`** --- the pre-call pool-count reads the previous pass counted as 48
references from the other direction. Two independent counts of the same defect,
by different methods, agreeing to within the sites the alignment can pair.

The remaining 395 temp/arg rows are a *position-dependent* rotation of
`t0`-`t9`: rotation by roughly +7 through +0x708..+0x804, by +4 through
+0x9F0..+0xAEC, by +5 or +6 through +0x8EC..+0x974. It is one ring whose phase
drifts, not ten independent colouring errors, which is why every source handle

### The residual is register naming, and it has one cause

Canonicalizing the one exchanged saved-register pair and the scratch-register
names leaves **296 of 616** differing words; the exchanged pair alone accounts
for 44 and the scratch ring for about 320. The saved-register census makes the
mechanism explicit: on the target side the exchanged pair carries 48 and 53
references, on the candidate side 101 and 48. The extra 48 are the pool-count
reads this unit performs *before* each loader call. The target colours those to
scratch temporaries; the candidate colours them to the same saved register that
carries the post-call index.

That single colouring decision is the whole cascade. Because the candidate
defines that saved register inside the entry block, its stack save must be
scheduled a slot earlier than the target's, which displaces the entry block by
one instruction from +0x88 onward and rotates the scratch-register ring for the
rest of the function. Instruction multisets are identical on both sides of the
displacement.

### The one improvement, and its mechanism

Assigning `y` before `x` in the sprite builder takes the retained candidate

not a coin flip: with the declared field order the candidate materializes the

### A measured phase knob, deliberately not adopted

The run did surface one real effect worth recording. Folding any two of a text

expression-tree effect, not a line-count one -- the prompt's embedded-assignment

re-phasing the scratch ring and buys no structure. Since it also changes the
AST -- unlike the earlier pass's line-order work, which deliberately preserved
it -- and since three equally-good positions mean the evidence cannot pick one,
it is recorded here rather than adopted. Revisit it only after the
saved-register exchange is fixed, at which point the three positions should
